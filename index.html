Hermes Express: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Hermes Express ("the game") is a single-player game for macOS by hansfigo ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data.

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • It does not connect to the internet while you play, and it does not use third-party SDKs that do.
  • Game progress is not uploaded anywhere. Any settings or progress stay on your Mac.
  • We do not track you and do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Links that open your browser

The game contains a link to this policy. Opening it launches your default web browser, which is then governed by the privacy policy of the website you visit, not by this one.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example because a future version adds online features), the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date, and the App Store privacy information will be updated to match.
